## Historical Observations

### 2025-11-13 - Pattern Analysis #2
**Summary**: TAO outperformed BTC with higher volatility yet clean 3-MA alignment.

**Findings**:
1. TAO LOW/MID crossover preceded HIGH MA by ~45 minutes, validating staged entries.
2. BTC failed to break above HIGH MA, signalling range exhaustion.
3. Volume spikes (via Binance ticker counts) clustered around TAO BUY alerts.

**Implications**:
- Maintain asymmetric risk: higher allocation to TAO when LOW> MID>HIGH while BTC stays flat.
- Expect TAO pullbacks to retest MID MA quickly; BTC pullbacks drift longer.

**Trading Insights**:
- Use `/3ma` snapshots to confirm trend alignment per coin before acting.
- Pair TAO signals with tighter stop-loss because of volatility overshoots.
- BTC better suited for scaling strategies; TAO for momentum bursts.

### 2025-10-19 - Pattern Analysis #1
**Summary**: Low volatility uptrend detected (BTC-only baseline)

**Findings**:

4. Evening market tends to stabilise near daily highs

**Implications**:

**Trading Insights**:
